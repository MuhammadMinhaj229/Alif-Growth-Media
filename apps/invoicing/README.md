# 1-Tap Digital Invoicing & Daily Cash Tracker Architecture

**Module:** `apps/invoicing`  
**Powered by:** Alif Growth Media  
**Target Repository Reference:** [`MuhammadMinhaj229/invofy`](https://github.com/MuhammadMinhaj229/invofy)  
**Production Deployment:** [invofy-kappa.vercel.app](https://invofy-kappa.vercel.app)

## Purpose & Scope
High-speed digital invoice generator that creates PDF bills, sends them straight to customer WhatsApp chats, and keeps a real-time ledger of Cash vs. UPI collections for daily closing.

## Key Features
- PDF invoice generation dispatched via WhatsApp in 1 tap
- Daily cash vs digital collection breakdown
- Customer billing ledger and balance tracking
